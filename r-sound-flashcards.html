How to Make the R Sound

Four little steps — practice them with Rex before the cards.

1

Circle lips

Make your lips into a small circle, like you're about to whistle or blow out a candle.

2

Tongue up & back

Point the tip of your tongue up and back, like a slide. Keep it floating — it doesn't touch anything!

3

Squeeze the sides

The sides of your tongue squeeze up against your back teeth, like your tongue is giving them a hug.

4

Turn your voice on

Growl like a lion: "Rrrr!" Feel the buzz in your throat. That's a real R!

Rex's trick

If R keeps turning into W, start with "eeee" and slowly slide your tongue back until it flips into "errrr." Or whisper "grrr" like a tiger first, then smile and say the word. Slow R is strong R — speed comes later.

For Grown-Ups

Quick ways to make practice work at home.

Little and often

Five minutes a day beats one long session. Run through the deck at breakfast or in the car and stop while it's still fun.

Use a mirror

Kids can't feel what their tongue is doing yet, but they can see it. Practice the "tongue up and back" slide in front of a mirror together.

Praise the try

R is one of the last sounds kids master — it's normal for it to take time. Cheer every attempt, model the word back correctly, and never make them repeat it ten times in a row.
